A Multimodal Dataset for Music-Induced Emotion and Micro-Behavior Analysis in Older Adults

Most emotion datasets feature young adults or actors. Older adults express emotion more subtly, and models trained on young faces often miss those signals. I led the construction of a therapist-annotated, de-identified multimodal dataset to fill this gap.

17older adults (60+)
200clips · ≈183K frames
1,326labeled micro-behaviors
4modalities: face · gaze · head · body
  • Designed an IRB-approved protocol: 12 music stimuli, self-reports of valence, arousal, liking and familiarity, with participants recruited from the community.
  • Built a 3-stage expert-to-label pipeline. A music therapist writes free-text observations, an LLM extracts structured timestamps, and a human validation pass turns them into a 12-class micro-behavior taxonomy.
  • Extracted de-identified features with OpenFace and OpenPose (action units, gaze, head pose, upper-body keypoints). Only derived features are shared, which protects participant privacy.
  • Funded by the USF CREATE Award and the Florida High-Tech Corridor Early-Stage Innovation Fund.

Chain-of-Modality (CoM) Prompting for Micro-Behavior Detection

Can an LLM read raw behavioral signals (gaze vectors, action-unit intensities, skeleton keypoints) and describe what a person is doing second by second, without task-specific training?

  • Proposed Chain-of-Modality prompting with GPT-4o. Modalities are reasoned about one at a time (gaze → face → body → head), and each step builds on the previous answer.
  • Zero-shot temporal localization of therapist-annotated behaviors reached R@1@0.3 = 70.0% and mIoU = 0.524.
  • Downstream mood-change prediction reached 0.88 accuracy from therapist-annotated behavior sequences.
  • Analyzed failure cases second by second, tracing error propagation from over-segmentation and mistimed cues. The analysis shows where LLM temporal reasoning breaks down.
Ongoing

Cold-Start Personalization with Adaptive Modality Selection

People express emotion through different channels. Some show it on the face, others through gaze or posture. I am studying how to choose the right modalities for a new person with little or no labeled data.

  • A zero-shot KNN warm-up for personalized modality selection reaches 0.770 balanced accuracy and closes 51% of the baseline–oracle gap.
  • Evaluating wearables (Fitbit Charge 6, Empatica EmbracePlus, Polar H10) for low-burden HRV and EDA sensing with older adults.
IEEE CBMS 2025

Multimodal Neonatal Pain Assessment in the NICU

Newborns cannot report pain. As a member of the engineering team on RPAL’s Neonatal Pain Assessment project, I work with USF Health clinicians on using facial expression, body movement and vital signs to detect and predict post-operative pain.

  • Processed multimodal NICU data (face, body movement, HR, respiration rate, SpO2).
  • LSTM pain classifier: 74% AUC, 67.59% mAP (5-fold CV). Benchmarked CNN+LSTM, ViViT and Transformer models for video-based detection.

Bridge2AI-Voice Scholar (NIH Common Fund program). Machine learning modeling and technical support for my team at the Bridge2AI-Voice Hackathon: 9 teams from USF, Weill Cornell, OHSU and WashU.

  • End-to-end audio ML pipeline: 130 engineered acoustic features, PCA and feature selection, Random Forest and XGBoost. Up to 93.2% accuracy.
